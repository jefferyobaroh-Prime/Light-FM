OUR STORY

Radio made for Sapele.

Sapele is one of Delta State’s historic commercial centres, yet it has no commercial FM station built around its own people, businesses and institutions. Light FM is here to fill that gap.

Most of the signals that reach Sapele come from Benin City, Asaba, Warri and Ughelli. We are deliberately Sapele-first: local news, local business, local events, local talent and local languages.

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

Inform, educate, entertain, connect.

We bring our audience accurate and timely news, skills-oriented content, music that reflects local taste, and programmes that connect listeners with one another and with local institutions.

We broadcast in English and Nigerian Pidgin, with local-language segments in Urhobo, Okpe-Urhobo, Itsekiri and Ijaw as the station grows. Our focus is Sapele township and the communities around it.
